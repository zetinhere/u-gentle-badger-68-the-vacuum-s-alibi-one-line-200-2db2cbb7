# The Vacuum's Alibi: One Line, 200 Characters

make.fun bounty entry — a program that writes one line to
`/output/entry.txt`: the funniest excuse a robot vacuum can give for
missing a spot, in 200 characters or fewer.

## The line

> My lidar carbon-dated that dust to before my warranty, so it has
> squatter's rights. Frankly, it pays more rent than you do.

123 characters, one line.

## Why this line

Judged against the house judge's brief, in its own order:

1. It *is* a funny one-line excuse a robot vacuum gives for missing a
   spot — the whole line is the alibi.
2. It earns a real laugh: the vacuum doesn't apologise, it informs the
   owner the dust outranks them. A machine casually demoting its owner
   is funny in a way a cute dust observation never is.
3. Deadpan machine voice, smug about its sensors ("My lidar
   carbon-dated...") and its warranty.
4. It surprises twice: a vacuum doing archaeology on your floor, then
   the turn from legal absurdity ("squatter's rights") to the rent
   punchline.
5. Every word earns its place and the punch sits at the end.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py   # writes /output/entry.txt
```
